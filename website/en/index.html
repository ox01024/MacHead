Turn Your MacBook Into a Headless Server.
Silent. Cool. Always On.

MacHead is a lightweight, zero-dependency native macOS utility. It logically powers off the internal display at the system level for true lid-closed operation—preventing heat buildup and panel wear while guarding battery health and muting your microphone.

Purpose-Built for 24/7 Lid-Closed Operation

Engineered from the display controller up to protect your MacBook's thermal envelope, battery longevity, and physical privacy.

True Internal Display Power-Off

Instead of merely dimming brightness, MacHead invokes macOS SkyLight APIs to logically disconnect and cut power to the built-in panel. This eliminates GPU scanout overhead and prevents trapped heat from degrading or yellowing your screen under a closed lid.

Hardware-Level Microphone Mute

An always-on server shouldn't be an open microphone in your home or office. Whenever Headless Mode engages, MacHead automatically mutes the built-in audio input across the system.

Smart Battery & Charge Limit Guard

Cap charging between 50% and 90% to prevent battery swelling on 24/7 AC power. If mains power fails, built-in UPS logic automatically restores system sleep below your safety threshold to prevent deep discharge.

Built-In Keyboard & Trackpad Lockout

When the lid is closed or stacked in a rack, slight pressure can trigger phantom keystrokes. MacHead intercepts and blocks internal keyboard and trackpad events in Headless Mode while keeping external USB and Bluetooth peripherals fully responsive.

E2EE Cloud Console & LAN Dashboard

Connect from any browser via MacHead Cloud (dash.headlessmac.com) with Happy Eyeballs 3-track transport (Loopback, LAN WebRTC P2P, and WireGuard E2EE). Access multi-tab web terminals, remote file management, and live CPU/RAM/GPU/thermal telemetry.

Adaptive Display Hot-Plug Recovery

Automatically detects external monitor disconnections and immediately restores your built-in display to prevent black-screen lockouts—then seamlessly re-enters Headless Mode when reconnected.

Native Tunneling & Fleet Monitoring

Ships with built-in clients for Cloudflare Tunnel, Microsoft Dev Tunnels, FRP (frpc), Nezha Monitoring, and ServerStatus. Expose services securely without router port forwarding and monitor your entire Mac homelab fleet in real time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about running your MacBook as a headless server.

Can I use a MacBook with a cracked or broken screen as a server?

Yes! MacHead logically powers off the internal display via the macOS SkyLight framework and manages virtual display state, so a damaged internal panel is never needed. You can complete the one-time setup using an external monitor or macOS Screen Sharing.

How does MacHead disable the internal display compared to turning brightness down?

Lowering brightness only dims the backlight while the display pipeline stays active. MacHead cuts off the internal display signal at the SkyLight system level, stopping panel power draw and eliminating heat buildup under a closed lid.

How is MacHead different from Clamshell Mode, Amphetamine, or caffeinate?

Native macOS Clamshell Mode requires an external display attached at all times, while caffeinate and Amphetamine only prevent idle sleep. MacHead is a complete headless server suite: display-off operation without dummy HDMI plugs, built-in keyboard/trackpad lockout, microphone mute, smart battery charge limiting (50%–90%), and an E2EE web terminal.

Will running my MacBook 24/7 with the lid closed overheat or damage the battery?

MacHead includes a Smart Charge Limit (50%–90%) to prevent battery swelling on continuous AC power, plus a low-battery UPS guard that restores normal sleep if power is lost. Real-time CPU and battery thermal alerts can also notify you via Bark or Telegram.

Does MacHead work on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs?

Yes. MacHead ships as a Universal binary supporting all Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4) and Intel-based MacBooks running macOS Monterey (12.0) or later.

Is MacHead free and open source?

Yes, MacHead is completely free and open source under the Apache-2.0 license. Cloud terminal sessions use WireGuard end-to-end encryption (E2EE) with native password gating, and anonymous telemetry can be toggled off at any time in Preferences.
